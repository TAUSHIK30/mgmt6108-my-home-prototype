# Prototype reflection notes

AI-assisted contribution, not a completed group reflection or an account of participant results.

The implementation separates evidence from simulation. A reconstructs the observed issue with an old-task preview and empty home summaries, but is clearly identified as simplified. B prioritises upcoming work, distinguishes optional work, uses the same fictional deadlines in task and Calendar views, and separates announcement scopes.

The underlying task data remain identical across variants. A exposes a full activity register, avoiding an artificial comparison where participants cannot obtain the answers at all. A bundled change can show a combined usability effect, not prove expectations or loss aversion caused it.

No participants have been recruited, no tests run, no improvement measured. Study mode is a local demonstration with consent, timer and debrief. It is not a full research data platform. Group members must contribute their actual source checks, ratings, decisions and reflections.
