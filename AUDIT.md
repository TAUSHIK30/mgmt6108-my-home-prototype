Recorded 31 October 2026 before local implementation. This record follows the user's request to build, so it is not claimed as a preregistration before the first build prompt. No student outcomes are reported.

Decision: identify upcoming coursework and choose the next activity.

Evidence: screenshots captured in this chat show Work To Do listing Overdue 15 with September activities including Optional Class Participation, home Calendar with no events, and home Announcements with no notices. Upcoming course information was inspected separately. The current prototype is a simplified reconstruction using fictional activities, not a pixel-perfect reproduction or a live eLearn connection.

1. Upcoming work: put a seven-day list before older tasks. Week 3 expectations/exposure and attention, slides 6, 49, 68–72. Predict improved identification of upcoming tasks.
2. Optional activities: separate them from verified required-overdue tasks. Week 4 framing/loss aversion, slides 45, 48. Predict better classification of obligations; monitor pressure.
3. Calendar coverage: show consistent course tasks and state the scope. Week 3 expectations. Predict fewer false nothing-is-due conclusions.
4. Announcement scope: separate institution and course notices. Week 3 interpretation/expectations. Predict easier discovery of exam notices.
5. Placement: put a seven-day summary at the top. Week 3 exposure/attention. Predict reduced search time. Related to finding 1 rather than independent causal evidence.

Provisional classification grey; no deliberate manipulation is claimed. Individual/group severity ratings remain uncollected. Proposed individual discussion rating 2/4 is not a group consensus.
